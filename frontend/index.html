02 — Our story

Born from a love of
unhurried mornings.

Serein began with a simple idea: create the kind of place we always searched for — warm, quietly beautiful, and deeply attentive to the little things.

What started as a six-table coffee bar has grown into a neighbourhood ritual. Our team of bakers, baristas and cooks still approaches each day the same way: with curiosity, care and a healthy respect for good ingredients.

“There is something lovely about a place that remembers how you take your coffee.”
— Mira & Arun, founders
